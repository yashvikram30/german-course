## 1 The exam at a glance

The **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1** has four parts, called *Prüfungsteile*. The three written parts (Hören, Lesen, Schreiben) are taken together in one sitting without a break. Speaking is a separate group exam, usually on the same day or within 14 days.

| Prüfungsteil | Time | Teile | Tasks | Raw points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hören** (listening) | about 20 min | 3 | 15 | 15 |
| **Lesen** (reading) | 25 min | 3 | 15 | 15 |
| **Schreiben** (writing) | 20 min | 2 | form (5) + message (1 text) | 15 |
| **Sprechen** (speaking) | 15 min, groups of up to 4 | 3 | introduce, ask and answer, request | 15 |

Written part: 65 minutes. Together with speaking the exam takes about 80 minutes. There is **no preparation time** for Sprechen, and dictionaries are not allowed anywhere. These figures follow the Goethe-Institut's *Durchführungsbestimmungen* (version of 1 September 2025) and its *Prüfungsziele und Testbeschreibung*.

### What each Teil asks

| Teil | Hören | Lesen |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Short everyday conversations. Pick a, b or c. Played **twice**. | Two short notes or emails. Is each statement *richtig* or *falsch*? |
| **2** | Public announcements (station, shop, bus). *Richtig* or *falsch*. Played **once**. | Ten small ads, in five pairs. Which ad of each pair fits the situation? |
| **3** | Phone messages. Pick a, b or c. Played **twice**. | Five very short signs or notices. *Richtig* or *falsch*? |

| Teil | Schreiben | Sprechen |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Fill in five gaps in a form, using the information in a short text. | Introduce yourself from keywords (*Name, Alter, Land, Wohnort, Sprachen, Beruf, Hobby*), spell a word, give a number. |
| **2** | Write a message of about **30 words** covering **three given points**. | Ask and answer questions with word cards (each candidate: two questions, two answers). |
| **3** | – | Make requests with picture cards and react to your partner's requests. |

## 2 How the points work

Each part is first marked in **raw points**, then converted. You do not need to remember the details, but you should know how to read your result.

| Prüfungsteil | Raw points | How they are given |
|---|---|---|
| Hören | 15 | 1 point per correct answer (Teil 1: 6 tasks, Teil 2: 4, Teil 3: 5) |
| Lesen | 15 | 1 point per correct answer (5 + 5 + 5) |
| Schreiben, Teil 1 | 5 | 1 point per correctly filled gap |
| Schreiben, Teil 2 | 10 | 3 points per content point (3 × 3) + 1 point for the style of the text |
| Sprechen | 15 | Teil 1: 3 points, Teil 2: 6 points, Teil 3: 6 points |

To get the official result, the raw points of each part are **multiplied by 1.66**, added up, and rounded to a whole number. The maximum is 100 points: 75 for the written part and 25 for speaking.

> **Total = (Hören + Lesen + Schreiben + Sprechen) × 1.66**, where each part is out of 15 (so the four parts add up to a maximum of 60 raw points).

| Official points | Grade |
|---|---|
| 100 – 90 | sehr gut |
| 89 – 80 | gut |
| 79 – 70 | befriedigend |
| 69 – 60 | ausreichend |
| 59 – 0 | nicht bestanden |

```merke
**You pass with at least 60 points out of 100 (60 %)** and only if you have taken all four parts. In raw points that means about **36 of 60**: 36 × 1.66 = 59.76, which rounds to 60. If you score fewer than 35 points in the written part, speaking can no longer save you, because even full marks in Sprechen would not reach 60.
```

### Worked example

| Part | Raw | × 1.66 |
|---|---|---|
| Hören | 11 / 15 | 18.26 |
| Lesen | 12 / 15 | 19.92 |
| Schreiben | 4 + 7 = 11 / 15 | 18.26 |
| Sprechen | 9 / 15 | 14.94 |
| **Total** | 43 / 60 | 71.38, rounded **71 = befriedigend, passed** |

### Quick converter

| Raw total (out of 60) | 30 | 33 | 36 | 40 | 45 | 50 | 55 | 60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Official points | 50 | 55 | 60 | 66 | 75 | 83 | 91 | 100 |
| Result | no | no | **pass** | pass | pass | gut | sehr gut | sehr gut |

(The rounded values come from raw × 1.66. A raw total of 35 gives 58, so it is a fail.)

## 3 Marking Schreiben, Teil 2

The examiners give points for **each of the three content points** (*Inhaltspunkte*) and for the **form of the text** (greeting and closing).

| Criterion | Points | What earns it |
|---|---|---|
| Each content point (×3) | **3** | Done completely and understandable |
| | **1.5** | Partly done: language or content problems, but still understandable |
| | **0** | Not done, or not understandable |
| Text type (greeting and closing) | **1** | Fitting greeting and closing (*Liebe Anna, … Viele Grüße*) |
| | **0.5** | Typical phrases missing or odd (for example no greeting) |
| | **0** | No fitting phrases at all |

Spelling mistakes are **only penalised when they stop the reader from understanding**. Only what is written on the answer sheet is marked, and the length is about 30 words: much less, and you probably miss a content point; much more gives you nothing extra.

```tipp
**Write one sentence per content point.** Open with a greeting, give each of the three points its own short sentence, and close with a greeting and your name. That already gives you about 30 words and the full 10 points are within reach, even with mistakes.
```

## 4 Marking Sprechen yourself

You cannot be marked by the page when you speak. Record yourself on your phone, listen back, and tick the checklist for each Teil. The examiners look at whether your answers are **appropriate and understandable**: a simple sentence with small mistakes still counts.

| Teil | Points | Self-check: can you honestly tick all of these? |
|---|---|---|
| **1** Introduce yourself | 3 | You covered all seven keywords. You can spell a word aloud letter by letter. You can say a phone number or other number clearly. |
| **2** Ask and answer | 6 | You made a real question from the word card (verb in position 1 or after the question word). You answered your partner's question with a full sentence. You did this twice each. |
| **3** Requests | 6 | You made a polite request (*Können Sie …? Bitte …! Ich möchte …*). You reacted to your partner's request (*Ja, gern. Natürlich. Es tut mir leid, das geht nicht.*). You did this twice each. |

Give yourself the points like this: Teil 1: one point for each of the three tick-groups. Teil 2 and 3: three points for the questions or requests, three points for the reactions, reduced if you stopped or could not be understood. Be strict: a real examiner is a stranger.

## 5 How to use the Modelltests

The four **Modelltests** in the *Prüfung* section are complete mock exams with the same parts as the real one: **Hören** (3 Teile), **Lesen** (3 Teile), **Schreiben** (2 Teile) and **Sprechen** (3 Teile).

1. **Use exam mode.** It runs the real timings (Hören about 20 minutes, Lesen 25, Schreiben 20, Sprechen 15), limits how often you can play the audio, and shows no answers or hints until the end. An untimed practice switch is available if you just want to work through a test.
2. **Hören:** the audio is a computer voice. In Teil 1 and 3 you may play each text **twice**, in Teil 2 only **once**. The real exam uses pictures in Teil 1; here the answers are short sentences instead.
3. **Schreiben and Sprechen need your honesty.** After the exam the app shows a model answer. Give yourself points with the table in section 3 (writing) and the checklist in section 4 (speaking). Say your speaking answers aloud during the exam; recording yourself helps.
4. **Learn from mistakes.** For every wrong answer read the explanation, then find the topic in chapters 1 to 12 and repeat it.
5. **Move on** when you reach **at least 45 raw points (75 official points)** in two tests in a row. Below 36 raw points, repeat the weak chapters before the next test.

```tipp
In the real exam you mark answers first in the booklet and then copy them to the answer sheet. You get about 5 minutes to copy. **Never leave a gap**: wrong answers cost nothing, a blank answer scores zero.
```

```kultur
**Landeskunde: where do I take the exam?** The Goethe-Institut and its partner centres offer *Start Deutsch 1* worldwide, in paper or digital form. In the digital version you work on a computer and the multiple-choice parts are marked automatically. Speaking is still on paper and face to face. The exam is for people aged 16 and over, and it can only be repeated as a whole. The certificate does not expire, but many offices want it to be recent.
```
